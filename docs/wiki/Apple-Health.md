# Apple Health

Only workouts recorded with GPS (outdoor walks, runs, rides) have routes.

> Pictures are simplified drawings of each screen with the button to press circled in red. Menu names change a little between app versions.

## iPhone

**Step 1.** Open **Health** and tap your **picture** (top right).

![Open **Health** and tap your **picture** (top right)](images/apple-1.png)

**Step 2.** Scroll down and tap **Export All Health Data**.

![Scroll down and tap **Export All Health Data**](images/apple-2.png)

**Step 3.** Tap **Export** and wait (it can take minutes).

![Tap **Export** and wait (it can take minutes)](images/apple-3.png)

**Step 4.** Tap **Save to Files** to keep `export.zip`.

![Tap **Save to Files** to keep `export](images/apple-4.png)

## Then upload it

Upload `export.zip`; only workout routes are read. Too big? Unzip it in Files and choose the `.gpx` files in `apple_health_export/workout-routes`.

See [Uploading to CrossPaths](Upload.md).
