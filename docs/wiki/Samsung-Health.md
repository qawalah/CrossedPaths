# Samsung Health



> Pictures are simplified drawings of each screen with the button to press circled in red. Menu names change a little between app versions.

## Android

**Step 1.** Open **Samsung Health** and tap **☰** or your profile → **Settings**.

![Open **Samsung Health** and tap **☰** or your profile → **Settings**](images/samsung-1.png)

**Step 2.** Tap **Download personal data**.

![Tap **Download personal data**](images/samsung-2.png)

**Step 3.** Tap **Download**. Files go to `Download/Samsung Health`.

![Tap **Download**](images/samsung-3.png)

**Step 4.** In **My Files**, long-press the *Samsung Health* folder → **Compress** to make a `.zip`.

![In **My Files**, long-press the *Samsung Health* folder → **Compress** to make a `](images/samsung-4.png)

## Then upload it

Upload the `.zip`. The routes are in the `location_data.json` files; everything else is skipped.

See [Uploading to CrossPaths](Upload.md).
