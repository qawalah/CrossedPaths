CrossPaths: When Paths Crossed

Short description (max 80)
--------------------------
See when you and a friend were in the same place, from your location timelines.

Full description (max 4000)
---------------------------
Ever wondered whether you and someone you know were in the same café, mall or street long before you met? CrossPaths compares your location history with a friend's and shows you every time your paths crossed.

HOW IT WORKS
- Export your Timeline from Google Maps (or build one from the location saved in your photos) and upload it.
- Connect with a friend by username, or scan each other's code when you're together.
- You both agree, and CrossPaths finds every time you were within 100 metres of each other within the same 15 minutes.

WHAT YOU SEE
- A summary first: how many times you crossed, on how many days, for how long in total, and your longest crossing.
- Where you cross most, by place and by city, with a heat map that shows your hot spots and breaks into individual crossings as you zoom in.
- When it happens: by month, time of day and day of the week.
- Three kinds of crossing: same place (you were both there), crossed paths (you passed through the same spot) and travelled together (same road, bus or train at the same time).
- Open any crossing to see where it was, and replay your own path in the hours around it.
- Filter by type, year, city, place or length, and sort by newest, oldest or longest.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Runs on a CrossPaths server you or someone you trust controls.
- Nothing is compared until both people agree to each check.
- You only ever see your own path. The other person sees only when and roughly where you crossed, never your route.
- Times are rounded to 15 minutes and places blurred to about 100 metres. Home and work areas are detected and left out automatically.
- Your uploaded timeline is encrypted and deleted after 24 hours, unless you choose to keep it for 7 days, 30 days or until you delete it.
- Flights are never matched.

ALSO
- Meet in person: scan a QR code, share an invite link or shake phones to connect and run the check in one go.
- My Timeline: browse your own history by year, month and day on a map, and search for every visit to a place.
- Light and dark themes with five accent colours.
- Give the app a main and a backup address for your server and it switches automatically.

CrossPaths needs a CrossPaths server. The server is free and runs in Docker; setup takes a few minutes on any Docker host or cloud server.
